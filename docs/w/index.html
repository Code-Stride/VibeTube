Why VibeTube

No ads, ever

Every video plays straight through. No pre-rolls, no mid-rolls, no banners.

Background play

Lock your screen and the audio keeps going, with lock-screen and Bluetooth controls.

Picture-in-Picture

Pop the video into a floating window and keep watching while you use other apps.

SponsorBlock built in

Sponsor reads, intros and self-promo are skipped automatically.

Download and watch offline

Save videos to your phone for flights, commutes or patchy signal.

Full quality, up to 4K

Lock any resolution you like instead of being pushed down to 360p.

Mini player, Shorts and Live

Keep browsing while a video plays, with Shorts and live streams supported.
