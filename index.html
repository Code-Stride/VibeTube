FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VibeTube safe to use?

Yes! VibeTube is completely open-source. You can review the entire codebase on GitHub. It doesn't require root access or any special permissions beyond what's needed for its features.

Do I need a Google account?

No! VibeTube works without any Google account. It uses the Piped API, a privacy-focused YouTube frontend, to fetch content.

Will my YouTube account get banned?

VibeTube doesn't connect to YouTube's official API with your account. It uses an alternative API (Piped), so there's no risk to your Google/YouTube account.

Is this available for iOS?

Currently, VibeTube is only available for Android. An iOS version may be considered in the future.

How does SponsorBlock work?

SponsorBlock uses a community-driven database where users submit timestamps of sponsored segments, intros, outros, etc. VibeTube queries this database and automatically skips those segments during playback.

Can I download videos for offline viewing?

Yes! You can download videos in any available quality (144p to 4K) and even download audio-only versions. Downloads are saved to your device's Downloads/VibeTube folder.
